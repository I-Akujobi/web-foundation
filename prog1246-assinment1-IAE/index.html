Intro to the Tenno

They were called Tenno. Warriors of blade and gun:
masters of the Warframe armor. Those that survived the
old war were left drifting among the ruins.
Now they are needed once more.

Allow the Lotus to guide you. She has rescued you from
your cryostasis chamber and given you a chance to survive.
The Grineer will find you; you must be prepared.
The Lotus will teach you the ways of the Warframes and the secrets to unlocking their powers.
